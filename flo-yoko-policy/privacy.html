Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Flo Yoko Operator is an internal content management tool used to prepare, review, schedule, and publish content for the Flo Yoko Instagram account. This policy explains what information the tool may access and how it is used.

Information We Access

When you authorize the app through Meta or Instagram, the app may access basic account information, account identifiers, media publishing permissions, comments, messages, or related metadata depending on the permissions you grant.

How We Use Information

We use authorized information only to manage the Flo Yoko account, publish approved content, review account activity, respond to interactions, and improve content operations. We do not sell personal information.

Data Sharing

We do not share user data with third parties except where required to operate the service, comply with law, protect rights, or use Meta and Instagram platform features authorized by the account owner.

Data Retention

We retain operational logs, publishing records, and related metadata only as long as needed for account management, security, compliance, and troubleshooting.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of data associated with Flo Yoko Operator by following the instructions on our Data Deletion page.
